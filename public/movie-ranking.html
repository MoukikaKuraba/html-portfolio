The Best Movies According to Aswini !!!

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Salaar

A ruthless protector returns to his violent roots in the city-state of Khansaar to help his childhood friend seize power.

KGF

A poor boy rises from the streets of Mumbai to become a feared gangster and kingpin of the gold mines, fulfilling a promise to his dying mother.

Animal

A deeply troubled man launches a violent, obsessive quest for vengeance to win his estranged father's love and validation.
